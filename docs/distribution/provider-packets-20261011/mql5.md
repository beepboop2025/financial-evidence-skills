## Remaining gate

Native MQL5/MQL4 implementation, compilation, terminal/validation testing, seller identity and Market agreement. Market rules restrict external links/promotional panels and third-party licensing/update systems; the browser-link companion cannot simply be submitted as a Market product. No compiled native utility was created in this pass.

A compliant design must resolve source attribution within Market rules, keep public input access independent of off-platform paid licensing, and pass the actual terminal workflow. The rules allow free products but still require a genuine native product and seller review.

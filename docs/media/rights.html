Source credit and reuse

Credit the data and the analysis.

Each edition identifies its original data publishers and the calculation performed by LiquiLens. Public access and our software licence do not replace source-specific reuse terms.

Suggested citation

Source: [original publisher(s), observation/reporting period]; calculation and chart: LiquiLens, “[edition title],” [publication date], [permanent edition URL].

Keep the chart’s source note, units, dates and limitations visible. Attribute underlying reported numbers to the original publisher, and our transformation or calculation to LiquiLens. Do not imply an original publisher, regulator or newsroom endorses the analysis.

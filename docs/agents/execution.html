Carry the reasoning into your system
Seiche establishes funding context. LiquiLens retains the observation dates behind commercial-paper conditions. Undertow identifies missing permissions and the cost of a hypothetical liquidation. Keep those roles distinct and retain the refusal as carefully as the fill. A passing policy check is not a forecast or a recommendation to trade.

Match the evidence to the question

QuestionEvidence to examineScope of this workflow
Are overnight dollar-funding conditions changing?SOFR, its upper percentile and volume, IORB, EFFR, reserves, Treasury cash, ON RRP and SRF operations.Seiche's nine-input funding packet, with per-series dates and explicit exceptions. No single composite stress score.
What is happening to U.S. government borrowing?Debt stock, budget flows, issuance and maturity structure.A separate fiscal question. The funding packet's Treasury cash balance is not a debt-stock or debt-sustainability measure.
Is the dollar appreciating or losing reserve share?A specified exchange-rate measure or dated reserve-currency dataset.Separate from short-term funding. This packet does not measure the dollar's broad exchange value or reserve share.
Can a named institution meet withdrawals?Institution-specific liquidity and balance-sheet evidence with known coverage and lags.System funding rates cannot answer this. LiquiLens's disclosure example covers one issuer and historical reporting periods.
What would it cost to exit a market position?Market depth, venue coverage and a specified size and horizon.Undertow supplies separate indicative BTC market-liquidity research where supported; it does not measure Treasury-market depth through this funding packet.

Try it in your existing tools

  1. Open the review, inspect its source dates and exceptions, then select Save evidence packet or Download CSV.
  2. Keep source, unit, observation date and missing-value state beside every number. The JSON packet retains the matching manifest and source notices.
  3. Use Excel, Python or DuckDB, or inspect the notebook and agent integrations. A native platform listing is not required to try the public workflow; each integration lists its own readiness.
  4. For a recurring job, inspect the funding automation kit. Preserve failure and stale-data states; a successful download does not make every input simultaneous.

Prepared 11 October 2026 IST. State: **packet ready full account required**. No new application, agreement or message was sent by this preparation work.

## Proposed recurring use

A treasury/data team joins a narrow table of daily EUR reference observations to its own research tables. The first offer is a free evaluation share with explicit source and vintage columns; its purpose is testing data ingestion and recurring use.
